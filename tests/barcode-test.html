Barcode tests (Phase 6)

Part 1 checks the helpers in js/barcode.js (check digit, size parsing, saved barcodes). Part 2 drives the real app in the frame below with the online lookup replaced by a stub, so no network is needed. Part 3 tests camera scanning with a drawn barcode standing in for the camera; it needs a browser with a built-in barcode reader (Chrome on a Mac or Android) running in real time, so headless Chrome with --virtual-time-budget fails it. Other browsers skip Part 3. Open through the web server (same-origin iframe). Your saved offers and saved barcodes are set aside at the start and put back at the end. Look for "ALL PASS".
